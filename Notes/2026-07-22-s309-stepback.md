## THE TOUR — own-eyes walk findings

### 1. HEADLINE: alias promotion rewrites the manuscript, demonstrated on the flagship sentence

At the writing beat, Tab-promoting "Consul" replaced the writer's word with the canonical name.
Link-all then did it six more times. The chapter now reads:

> "The Hegemony **The Consul** sat on the **Spaceship Balcony** of his **Ebony Spaceship** and played
> Rachmaninov's Prelude in C-sharp Minor on an ancient but well-maintained **The Consul's Steinway**
> while great, green, **Saurians** things surged and bellowed in the swamps below."

Ungrammatical in four places — while the dock directly below displays the pristine sentence for
contrast. Mechanism: name/case matches are case-preserving (S307's GUARANTEE), but **alias matches
resolve the visible text to the canonical name**. This is exactly the question held for Captain since
S307 ("should a genuinely different alias keep the writer's text?"), now with evidence on the tour's
own climax. My recommendation: **the chip label keeps the writer's matched text for ALL match kinds**
— identity lives in the link target, not the label. Consequence: the tour sentence stays pristine, and
real manuscripts stop being silently rewritten by link-all. Needs Captain's ruling because it changes
the autolink contract (THE GUARANTEE tests extend, existing chips unaffected — label is display).

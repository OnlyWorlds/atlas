## Traps hit this session (worth keeping)

- **The reorder surfaced a shipped bug by walking the promise through the
  stack.** The pin beat asks the user to click the swamps; the zone ate the
  click. No test imagined it — the design consumed existing zone behavior. Fix
  the seam, don't work around it in the tour.
- **A raw map click is async placement.** The e2e "no pin" failure was a race:
  `.click()` resolves before `createPin` lands. Poll for the pin before reading
  it. The pixel→storage mapping of a click is not worth asserting — force the
  pin to the zone centroid via the store and test the GATE, which is what's new.
- **pinInZone is unit-tested on synthetic geometry; the bundle geometry is
  guarded separately.** The baked pin sits inside the baked swamps, locked by a
  test, so watch-mode/resume never dead-ends. A re-bake that moves either out of
  overlap fails that test loudly.

## The proxy-lie trap (worth keeping)

My first "own-eyes" check that clicking the zone places a pin used synthetic
`dispatchEvent(MouseEvent)` — which does NOT reproduce Leaflet's real click
delegation and probably placed via the map-background path. A convenient proxy
that behaved differently from the user's click, i.e. a green that meant nothing.
The gate's (wrong) HIGH is what forced a REAL Playwright click, which is what
actually verified the feature. Verify in the user's real input space; a
`dispatchEvent` is not a click.

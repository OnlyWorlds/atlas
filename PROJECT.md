## Charter (signed)

*Signed by the Captain; changed only by him. The 2026-10-18 change below is his (the catch-up, h20 #6839).*

**Outcome.** Atlas at atlas.onlyworlds.com is the place anyone sees, explores, creates and edits an OnlyWorlds world, on any desktop browser: the folder is the world, OnlyWorlds online is an optional sync target, and nothing a person makes is ever lost or quietly changed.

**Current milestone.** **The journey** (OW Journey, h57): a stranger gets from onlyworlds.com into a world of their own in Atlas and back out, every Atlas step of it (A1 to A19, `Forge/Notes/2026-10-15-journey-atlas.md`) walked by the crew and then by you as the new user, the pinches it finds fixed. Before it: **Worlds in your browser** (1.17), done.

**Crew.** Temper leads (the tool, the build, its record). Skeld (the folder format and keel; reads every contract that touches identity or the format). Boss (deploys and the edge numbers, by letter). Kael (cards of Atlas's tech, reviewed by Temper). Hangout: Atlas (h20).

**The lead decides alone.**
- The build order inside the milestone, and how a bout is gated and tested.
- Fixes, refactors and engine changes that keep what a person sees and keeps the same, measured and gated.
- Wording of errors and help, within the voice guide.
- Releasing: commit, push and the deploy brief to Boss when the gates are green (your standing word for this build list).
- Asks to Skeld, Boss and Kael.

**Always goes to you.**
- Anything that could lose, move or change a person's data in a new way (a migration, a delete, a new place worlds live).
- A new feature or a change to how Atlas behaves that a person would notice and did not ask for (design).
- Anything Atlas sends or counts about people (the usage note stays parked until you reopen it).
- Public words beyond the changelog: announcements, the onlyworlds.com pages, posts.
- Spend.

**The record this project keeps** (not items): `PROGRESS.md` (CURRENT STATE and each session), `DESIGN_LAWS.md` (the numbered conventions), the public changelog (`src/changelog/changelog.data.ts`), the dated decision notes and handovers in `Forge/Notes/`, the repo's history.

**Retired when items land**: the build list in `Forge/Notes/Archive/2026-10-04-s368-handover.md`, the "NEXT" lines of PROGRESS CURRENT STATE, and Atlas's live-thread line in Temper's memory index (all become items or pointers to them).
